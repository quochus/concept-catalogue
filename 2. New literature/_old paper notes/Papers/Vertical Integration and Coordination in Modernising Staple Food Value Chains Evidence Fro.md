## Takeaways

- Studies how domestic rice millers in Myanmar expand into services beyond milling, a midstream gap in literature focused on FDI and high-value foods.
- Uses miller and farmer survey data with tests controlling for mill and farmer characteristics.
- Modern mills engage more in vertical integration/coordination than traditional mills, especially post-farm.
- Farmers using modern mills more often receive complementary services and adopt modern practices, but large farms are reached more than small and medium ones.
- Read from the IFPRI working-paper summary; journal version just alerted.

## Abstract

Examines vertical integration and coordination by modern and traditional rice mills in Myanmar, finding co-development of modern processing and vertical integration, with modern mills providing more services (especially post-farm) and their farmer clients more likely to adopt modern practices, though reach skews to larger farms.
