## Takeaways

- Proposes a six-domain readiness framework (production, market engagement, post-harvest handling/storage, labour, institutional embeddedness, strategic orientation) with hierarchical composite indices and a validation step.
- Applied to a baseline survey of 172 fruit and vegetable farmers in Makueni County, Kenya.
- Post-harvest handling and storage is critically low and the main bottleneck; market engagement is shallow and institutional embeddedness (finance, infrastructure) weak despite fairly high training levels.
- Readiness is fragmented: strength in one domain does not translate into overall system readiness.

## Why it matters

The domain-based readiness index is a transferable diagnostic for the FSDS toolbox and for framing post-harvest and finance gaps among nutrient-dense value chain actors in the MSME finance PoD and Son La cold-chain study.
