## C27 Market systems development (5 sources now; target 8–12)

| Priority | Source | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ruffer, T., & Wach, E. (2013). *Review of Making Markets Work for the Poor (M4P) evaluation methods and approaches* (Working Paper 41). DFID; Itad. | The main critical review of the evidence. It covers 32 programme documents and finds weak assessment of systemic change, data quality and theories of change. Gives the Debates section a basis. |
| 1 | Itad / BEAM Exchange. (2017). *The impact of programmes that use market systems approaches* (annual evidence review of the BEAM Evidence Map, ~100 documents). https://beamexchange.org/resources/1011 *(verify exact title and authors)* | Evidence synthesis: stronger evidence for growth and access than for poverty reduction. |
| 1 | Warner, B., & Loveridge, D. (n.d., c. 2017). *Synthesis review of DFAT-funded market systems development initiatives*. Australian DFAT. *(verify year)* | Donor portfolio review including Asia (Cambodia, Laos, Timor-Leste programmes). Finds credible headline results but weak evidence of systemic change. |
| 2 | Bekkers, H., Miehlbradt, A., & Roggekamp, P. (2008). How to assess if markets work better for the poor: Experiences from the Katalyst Project in Bangladesh. *Enterprise Development & Microfinance, 19*(2), 120–136. https://doi.org/10.3362/1755-1986.2008.012 | Peer-reviewed source for the Katalyst case (currently cited only from the Springfield synthesis). |
| 2 | Jones, L., & Miehlbradt, A. (2009). A 20/20 retrospective on enterprise development: In search of impact, scale and sustainability. *Enterprise Development & Microfinance, 20*(4), 304–322. https://doi.org/10.3362/1755-1986.2009.034 | History: four stages from credit to systems and inclusive-systems approaches. Strengthens the Definitions narrative. |
| 2 | Meyer-Stamer, J. (2006). Making market systems work? For the poor? *Enterprise Development & Microfinance, 17*(4), 21–32. https://doi.org/10.3362/0957-1329.2006.041 | Early critique: M4P needs clearer concepts of market failure and poverty. |
| 3 | Beignet, P., & Davies, G. (2019). Crossfire: Many organizations say they are doing M4P but are they really using the approach to its full potential? *Enterprise Development & Microfinance, 30*(4), 228–233. https://doi.org/10.3362/1755-1986.30-4.CF *(verify author list)* | Documents the renaming from M4P to MSD and the debate about diluted practice. |
| 3 | The Springfield Centre. (2015). *The operational guide for the Making Markets Work for the Poor (M4P) approach* (2nd ed.). SDC; DFID. *(verify)* | The practitioner standard; already named as an anchor in the Backlog. |
