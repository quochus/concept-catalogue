## Takeaways

- Mixed evidence base: interviews and workshops with changemakers in seven countries plus EU level, bibliographic mapping, a researcher survey and a scoping review.
- Identifies five recurring barriers to food system transformation: entrenched power, difficult policy environments, polarisation, fragmentation, and evidence ill-suited to implementation.
- Sets four investment priorities: more applied research, stronger evidence–decision links, long-term collaboration, and more effective narratives.
- Argues for targeted, longer-term, explicitly transformative funding rather than short project cycles. Full PDF embargoed until 2026-10-22 (only the record description was read).

## Why it matters

Its five-barrier typology (power, policy environment, polarisation, fragmentation, evidence gap) is a ready comparison frame for the institutional-change mechanisms in the Vietnam FST manuscript and for diagnosing bottlenecks in the ACF progress framework.

## Abstract

Report on why European and UK food systems underperform on health, climate and equity. Drawing on changemaker interviews/workshops across seven countries and the EU, bibliographic mapping, a researcher survey and a scoping review, it names five barriers and four investment priorities for funders.
