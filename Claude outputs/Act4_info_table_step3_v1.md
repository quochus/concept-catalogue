# Activity 4 – Step 3: revised table of information to be collected (draft for 2.2.3)

Working draft, 2026-11-05. Structure: A. scale and commodity profile; B1–B4 technical areas (cold storage systems; temperature monitoring; traceability; safety and quality management); C. adoption capacity. **(new)** = item not in the current table. **[Act x: …]** = already collected by another activity; use those data and do not ask again unless missing.

| Act 04-01 to 04-03 | KII | **Supply-side actors** (3): cooperative, agribusiness/processor, logistics operator with cold storage, quality control or traceability experience | **A. Scale and commodity profile** <br>- Main commodity groups handled (vegetables; main fruits) and chain stage of the respondent **(new)** <br>- Volumes and seasonality by commodity [Act 1: Act 01-06, 07/08, 09/10] <br>**B1. Cold storage systems** <br>- Facilities and equipment by commodity: pre-cooling, cold storage, ripening rooms, dry storage, refrigerated vehicles; year installed, owner, operator, working or not, capacity <br>- Use in peak and low season; shortages; periods of underuse and why <br>- Temperature, humidity and storage duration needed for main commodities; who sets them <br>- Point in the chain where cooling or storage is most needed (farm, collection point, packing house, center, market) **(new)** <br>- Use of or interest in low-cost, modular or off-grid options (plastic crates, evaporative coolers, solar cold rooms) **(new)** <br>- Energy source (grid, solar, diesel backup), electricity reliability and cost; losses from power cuts <br>**B2. Temperature monitoring** <br>- How temperature and humidity are monitored (manual, data loggers, sensors/IoT), what is recorded and how records are used <br>- Problems with monitoring devices, data accuracy or connectivity **(new)** <br>**B3. Safety and quality management** <br>- Sorting, grading, washing, packing and packaging practices and equipment (incl. crates, cartons, labelling) <br>- Certification held (VietGAP, GlobalGAP, HACCP, organic): status, cost, difficulties **(HACCP new)** <br>- Quality and safety testing (pesticide residues, microbial): who tests, where, how often <br>- Standard procedures for cold storage, transport, monitoring and traceability <br>**C. Adoption capacity** <br>- Technologies lacking; barriers to adoption or expansion (investment and operating cost, electricity, demand, skills, maintenance, suitability, volume, space) <br>- Gaps that cause most problems for quality, losses, shelf-life or market access [losses and buyer requirements: Act 1] <br>- Use of provincial support (cold storage, processing, traceability, certification): awareness, applications, support received, barriers to access **(new)** <br>- Own financial capacity for technology investment [credit and lending terms: Act 2] <br>- Breakdowns; access to maintenance, spare parts and technical support <br>- Staff skills; skills lacking; source of technical know-how (extension, suppliers, buyers, projects) **(source new)** <br>- Technologies and services expected from the logistics center (essential vs desirable); technical conditions; integrated vs individual services [services wanted: Act 1: Act 01-01/02, 06] <br>- Willingness to pay: which services, pricing basis (VND/kg, VND/t, VND/t/day, per trip, monthly), approximate fee [general WTP: Act 1] <br>- Preferred operating arrangement for shared equipment and cost sharing **(new)** [ownership models: Act 5] |
