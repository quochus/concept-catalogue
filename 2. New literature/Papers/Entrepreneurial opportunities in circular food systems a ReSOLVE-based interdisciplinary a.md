## Takeaways

- Systematic and bibliometric review of 68 articles (Scopus/WoS 2011–2025) plus 10 interviews with circular food entrepreneurs (Vietnam/Malaysia-based authors).
- Maps entrepreneurial opportunities onto all six ReSOLVE mechanisms (Regenerate, Share, Optimise, Loop, Virtualise, Exchange) to address food loss and waste.
- Opportunities emerge from interaction of micro entrepreneurial capabilities, meso value-chain collaboration and macro policy/institutional support.
- Authors flag limited sample and citation coverage as constraints on generalisability.

## Why it matters

ReSOLVE offers a ready-made coding scheme for circular-economy activities in FST 2026 survey coding, and the micro–meso–macro framing supports the APEC low-emission incentives note.

## Abstract

Examines how circular economy principles and entrepreneurial opportunities, framed by the ReSOLVE model, can turn food system inefficiencies in developing economies into regenerative and inclusive growth, via a systematic/bibliometric review of 68 articles and ten entrepreneur interviews.
