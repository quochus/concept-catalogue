## Takeaways

- Quantifies Hanoi's fresh-vegetable supply using multiple data sources and social network analysis of intermediary networks.
- Supply draws from all of northern Vietnam and parts of China; local (within-Hanoi) production still ~46% of supply.
- Most local vegetables pass through intermediaries (wholesalers, retailers, street vendors) that improve supply efficiency.
- Recommends food policy formally recognise middlemen, especially street vendors.

## Why it matters

Flow volumes and channel mapping for northern Vietnam vegetables give context for the Son La cold chain/logistics market feasibility (Son La as a supply zone for Hanoi) and the APEC value chain connectivity note.

## Abstract

Case study quantifying Hanoi's fresh-vegetable supply system using SNA and mixed methods; local production supplies 46% but flows mostly via intermediaries, and the supply area spans northern Vietnam and parts of China. Calls for policies recognising middlemen and street vendors.
