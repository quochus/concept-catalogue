# Activity 4: review of questions and respondents

*Working note for discussion, 2026-11-05. Based on ADB_Tools_Final_v2, ADB_Ethic_IRB_Protocol_v4 and the ToR (Appendix A). Proposals only; nothing has been changed in the tools.*

**Question codes.** T = tool, A = activity part, then the question's position in that part as printed in the tools (bullets counted in order). Example: T1-A4.3 = Tool 1, Act 4 part, third question. Sub-bullets of one question are not counted separately. In Tool 1, A1 has 14 questions plus 6 under "Potential demand for logistics center services", coded A1.15–A1.20.

### 3.3 Gaps and points to check

1. **[FLAG] Existing cold storage and logistics operators in Son La.** They are covered by Tool 1 ("logistics and transport operators"), but Tool 1 asks them as users, not as service providers. Nothing asks what they charge, their occupancy, or why facilities are underused, apart from T1-A4.3. This is the most direct evidence on whether a new center would be used.
2. **[FLAG] Electricity supplier.** Energy reliability is asked only of users (T1-A4.9) and providers (T6). No interview with the power utility is planned. Decide whether user reports are enough.
3. **[FLAG] Which Son La departments to interview after the 2025 reorganization.** Growing-area codes, VietGAP and traceability may sit with sub-departments of DAE (crop production and plant protection; quality management) or with another department. Needs checking against current Son La decisions before fieldwork.
4. **[FLAG] Interview count.** Protocol v4 gives 8–10 KIIs for Activity 4. Act 4 parts are also asked in the 15–18 Act 1 supply- and demand-side KIIs. Decide whether those count toward the 8–10.
5. **[FLAG] Private sector participation.** The ToR asks Output 4 to "highlight opportunities for private sector participation in technology deployment and operation of the logistics hub". The analysis in Protocol v4 does not list it. T6-A4.6 (business models) and T1-A4.14 (willingness to pay) collect part of this.
