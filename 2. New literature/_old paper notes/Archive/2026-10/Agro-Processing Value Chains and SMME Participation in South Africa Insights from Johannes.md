## Takeaways

- Mini scoping review of 40 sources (2020–2025: 28 peer-reviewed, 8 policy documents, 4 institutional reports) on SMMEs in Johannesburg's urban food system.
- Agro-processing has strong enterprise and job-creation potential, but SMMEs are held back by finance, food-safety compliance, infrastructure, logistics and market access constraints.
- Opportunities: niche urban markets, supplier development programmes, shared infrastructure and cooperative models.
- Evidence base is thin and mostly qualitative case studies, with few firm-level or distribution/retail analyses.

## Why it matters

Gives a compact constraint/opportunity list (finance, compliance, shared infrastructure, supplier development) to benchmark midstream MSME barriers in the MSME finance PoD and the Son La SME training design.
