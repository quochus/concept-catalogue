# Discuss

Where we talk through concepts. Start a note here (or just chat with Claude) about a concept, a question, or a passage you read.

- Each discussion is a dated note: `YYYY-MM-DD <topic>.md`.
- When a discussion is worth keeping, ask Claude to **save it to the concept**: it becomes a sub-page in `3. Concepts/<Concept>/Discussions/`, with the question, main points, where you landed (your conclusions), open questions and new sources, and is linked from the concept entry.
- Notes that are not about a concept yet stay here until you decide. Raw daily notes go in `1. Daily/`.
