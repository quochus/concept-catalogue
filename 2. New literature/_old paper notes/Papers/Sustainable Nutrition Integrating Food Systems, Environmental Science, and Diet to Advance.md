## Takeaways

- Perspective (NIH authors) proposing 'sustainable nutrition' as a unifying concept spanning lifespan nutrition and food system resilience.
- Nutritional Ecology framework: nutrition as the product of interactions among biology, diets, food systems and environment.
- Argues chronic-disease and environmental outcomes of diets should not be assessed separately.
- Calls for systems mapping, network analysis, modelling and multiscale data integration to find trade-offs and leverage points.

## Why it matters

A recent, citable framing for the diet-centred food systems manuscript, supporting the argument that diet outcomes should anchor food systems diagnostics.

## Abstract

Perspective arguing nutrition research must extend to food systems and environments, proposing 'sustainable nutrition' and a Nutritional Ecology framework, and recommending integrated systems-based, multidisciplinary research and modelling.
