## Takeaways

- Qualitative case study: 20 interviews across eight Victory Garden initiatives (municipal, community, school) in wartime Ukraine.
- Analysed against six food security dimensions (availability, access, utilisation, stability, agency, sustainability).
- Gardens contributed most to availability and access; overlapping access mechanisms (municipal land, seeds, household plots, family networks) acted as redundancy-based resilience.
- Utilisation relied on institutions such as schools, hospitals and military networks; school gardens emphasised food literacy.

## Why it matters

A concrete conflict-setting example of community food production and redundancy as resilience for the ANZVF concept note on food systems and peacebuilding in fragile settings.
