## Abstract

Draws on the Multi-Level Perspective and New Institutional Economics, secondary data and multi-stakeholder FGDs to analyse institutional drivers and barriers to adopting and scaling circular rice value chains in the Mekong Delta. A robust institutional lock-in, from fragmented legal and policy frameworks and obsolete informal structures, perpetuates the linear rice regime; dismantling it requires stronger formal regulations, shifting informal norms, and empowering intermediary institutions.
